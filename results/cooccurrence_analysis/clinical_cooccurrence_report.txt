2. CLINICAL INSIGHTS & METHODOLOGICAL IMPLICATIONS FOR GENOMIC SCREENING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A. Subclass Confounding vs. Independent Co-segregation:
   - Standard clinical ontologies like MONDO nest specific anatomical lesions under
     parent groups. For example, Atrial Septal Defect overlaps perfectly with Congenital
     Heart Disease (Jaccard = 0.5816, Overlap = 82/82). Methodologically, running synergy
     screens between parent and child groups represents redundant circular testing.
   - In contrast, Atrial Septal Defect (ASD) & Ventricular Septal Defect (VSD) represent
     true distinct lesion co-segregation (Jaccard = 0.4327, Overlap = 45 cases, p < 0.01).
     Finding synergy here isolates modifiers coordinating septal morphogenesis.

B. Inter-System Pleiotropy (Cross-Organ Phenotypes):
   - Highly powered, statistically robust co-occurrences emerge across different organ systems:
     * Congenital Heart Disease & Hypothyroidism (Overlap = 83 cases, p = 3.65e-04)
     * Congenital Heart Disease & Sleep Apnea Syndrome (Overlap = 56 cases, p = 0.017)
     * Hypothyroidism & Sleep Apnea Syndrome (Overlap = 55 cases, p = 0.024)
   - Biologically, this means that systemic modifiers (such as epigenetic writers like
     DNMT3A or interferon immune regulatory factors) could theoretically drive pleiotropic
     resilience across highly distinct tissues (cardiac cushions, thyroid metabolism, and airway soft-tissue).
   - This completely justifies expanding your pipeline beyond anatomically restricted modules
     to evaluate highly co-segregating cross-organ disease networks!
